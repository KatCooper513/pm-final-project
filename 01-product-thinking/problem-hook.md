# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** RouteLogic
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** If the company does nothing, it will lose market position. Productivity will slow, users and customers will be frustrated, find shortcuts or work arounds outside the platform.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Users will find workarounds, sending email which is less secure and with less oversight.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** We have an elevated security risk running on an outdated software version and server os, with recurring downtime for maintenance, users are finding unsecure workarounds.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** We need to upgrade to the latest software version and server os now to be in compliance and to take advantage of the leaner, more streamline features, increasing productivity, analytics, security, reliability, resiliency and satisfaction among our system engineers, users and customers.
