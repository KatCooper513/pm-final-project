05 · Strategy

The tool coordinators built a workaround for.

Problem

Real-time fleet coordinators abandon RouteLogic mid-shift because live dispatch is buried under admin panels built for HQ. They fall back to spreadsheets and phone calls; the largest enterprise account is trialling a leaner competitor that calls RouteLogic "an administrative burden."

Value Proposition

Velocity gives coordinators one focused, real-time view — live vehicle status, exceptions, and one-tap reroute — so the tool becomes the fastest way to run the shift instead of the slowest.

Hypothesis

We believe a streamlined real-time dispatch view for fleet coordinators will increase daily active use during shifts, measured by a +15pt lift in coordinator DAU within 30 days, without reducing HQ admin feature usage.

06 · Research

Where coordinators peel off — and why.

Competitive Analysis / Workaround

Coordinators cope by running the shift in spreadsheets and group texts, opening RouteLogic only to reconcile afterwards. Leaner competitors win with a mobile-first dispatch board; RouteLogic wins on depth but loses on speed at the moment of dispatch.

Journey Map Insight

Velocity surfaces a live dispatch board on open, before the coordinator reaches for the workaround — intercepting the moment of misery rather than reconciling after it.

PRD Highlights

A dedicated real-time dispatch view: live vehicle map, exception queue, and one-tap reroute, defaulting to the coordinator's assigned fleet.

No changes to existing admin/finance modules in V1 — the view is additive and preserves enterprise depth.

10 · Story

What this project taught me.

Friction & Aha Moment

The aha moment was realising the fix was subtraction, not features — coordinators did not need more power, they needed the live view unburied.

The hardest part was protecting enterprise depth while making the frontline view the default.

Key Takeaways / Next

Biggest takeaway: for B2B tools, the workflow at the moment of decision beats feature breadth.

Next, I would instrument the reroute flow and A/B test exception-alert timing to cut time-to-action further.
